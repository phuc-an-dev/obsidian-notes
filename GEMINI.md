# Project Instructions: Obsidian Second Brain

## Directory Structure & Organization
- **Root Mandate**: ALL knowledge notes, projects, and resources MUST reside within the `Second Brain/` directory.
- **Forbidden Paths**: Do NOT create or maintain a root-level `01-Knowledge/` or any other note-related folders outside of `Second Brain/`.
- **Note Placement**: Every new note must be categorized into the appropriate subdirectory within `Second Brain/01-Knowledge/` (e.g., `aws`, `react`, `java-spring`, etc.).

## Note Standards
- Follow the conventions defined in `NOTE_CONVENTION.md` for content structure and metadata.
- Always use the 16-section template for technical notes.
- Use Vietnamese for explanations and English for technical terms/code.
- NO emojis allowed in notes.
